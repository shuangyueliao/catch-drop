# Catch Drop — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Catch apples, oranges and rare golden apples with your basket. Chain catches
for combo multipliers, dodge bombs, and beat your best score. Simple, juicy,
addictive.

## Details

- Genre: Arcade / Casual / Action
- Controls: Mouse / Touch (drag to move the basket). Keyboard: ←/→.
- Features: Combo multiplier scoring (up to x1.8), 3 lives, bomb hits stun the
  basket for 0.5s, dropping 5 fruits resets the combo, rising speed and spawn
  rate over time, best-score saving, mute toggle, procedural graphics and
  WebAudio sounds (catch / gold / bomb / miss), single-file HTML5 (no external
  assets), works offline once loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. No frameworks, no tracking.
